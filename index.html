LASCE — Institutional Scientific Web Platform

Solar Astrophysics and Space Weather Laboratory · UCR

Visit Production

LASCE is a full-stack institutional and scientific-data platform developed for the Solar Astrophysics and Space Weather Laboratory of the University of Costa Rica. The platform provides a bilingual public portal for presenting research areas, scientific publications, news, collaborations, researchers, scientific instruments, space-weather projects, and the ROSAC radio observatory.

Implemented as a pnpm and Turborepo monorepo using TypeScript, Next.js 16, React 19, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Redis, BullMQ, MinIO, InfluxDB, and a background worker in Python 3.13.

Key Contributions: Complete CMS suite for news and ROSAC observatory (create, edit, delete workflows, Prisma transactions, MinIO uploads, and optimistic concurrency patterns), standardized management and confirmation modals, accessibility safeguards (axe-core, WCAG), and test automation across Playwright E2E and Vitest.

06. Engineering Philosophy

Mindset, Attitude & Engineering Culture

Syntax and framework mastery come with practice, but attitude, resilience, and engineering rigor are what truly define a high-impact software engineer.

End-to-End Ownership & Accountability

Solutions over excuses

I don't walk away with "it works on my machine" or "that's someone else's ticket". I take pride in end-to-end ownership: deeply understanding product requirements, building robust tests, and verifying production stability.

Intellectual Humility & Fast Learning

Feedback as a catalyst

I am honest about what I don't know and always open to learning from experienced engineers. I actively embrace rigorous Code Reviews—they are the fastest vehicle to elevate code quality and personal engineering depth.

Resilience & Growth Mindset

Persistence through complexity

When a complex technical bug or algorithmic challenge emerges, I don't get frustrated; I treat it as empirical feedback showing exactly where to focus next. Discipline and consistency consistently outpace talent alone.

Proactive Communication & Teamwork

Software is a collaborative craft

Great software comes from clear communication and empathy. I document design decisions, raise blockers early before they cause delays, and write clean code with the next maintainer in mind.
