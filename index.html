[PKI / CRYPTO]
Digital Signatures & PKI · Research Project
Oct 2026 – Present
GitHub Oct 2026 – Present

PAdES-LTA Renewer — Long-Term Digital Signature Preservation

GitHub

Co-developed a research project for the Software Quality course (CI-0140) at the University of Costa Rica: an open-source engine and web service that renews timestamps on digitally signed PDF documents to preserve their legal validity over the long term (PAdES-B-LTA, ETSI EN 319 142), in line with Costa Rica's Digital Signature Law (Law 8454). Implemented an RFC 3161 client for the SINPE Time Stamping Authority of the Central Bank of Costa Rica, a PKI inspector that rebuilds X.509 certificate chains up to the national root CA and checks revocation through OCSP and CRL, and an incremental PDF injector that adds /DSS evidence and document timestamps without invalidating existing signatures, all served through a minimal Flask web interface.

Python Flask RFC 3161 / ASN.1 X.509 / OCSP / CRL pypdf pytest
